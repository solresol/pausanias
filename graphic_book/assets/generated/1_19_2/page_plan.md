## Key places, events, and objects

- The Athenian place called the Gardens, plausibly in the Ilissos sanctuary landscape beyond the Olympieion; the exact sanctuary site has not been securely found.
- A temple of Aphrodite for which Pausanias says no local account was recorded.
- A separate square, Herm-like Aphrodite standing near the temple, identified by inscription as Heavenly Aphrodite and the eldest of the Fates.
- The celebrated Aphrodite in the Gardens by the sculptor Alcamenes, praised as one of the few Athenian objects especially worth seeing.
