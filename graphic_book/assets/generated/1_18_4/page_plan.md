# Page plan — Pausanias 1.18.4

- Passage ID: `1.18.4`.
- Key places, events, and objects: the descent into lower Athens; the Athenian sanctuary of Serapis introduced under Ptolemaic influence; Alexandria's pre-eminent Serapeion; the older Memphite sanctuary beside the Apis burial landscape at Saqqara; restricted entry until the burial of Apis; and the nearby agreement of Theseus and Peirithous to travel to Lacedaemon and then Thesprotia.
- Main visual: a richly rendered, historically informed reconstruction of an Athenian Serapis sanctuary court in the lower city, combining Greek colonnaded architecture with restrained Egyptian cult details. The Acropolis rises behind to orient the descent from the upper city. Serapis' cult image is visible inside the shaded sanctuary, while worshippers and attendants provide scale without turning the scene into a generic temple view.
- Insets: (1) a textured painted-relief orientation view spanning Athens, Alexandria, the Nile Delta, Memphis, and Saqqara, used only as a subordinate locator; (2) the guarded Memphite/Saqqara sacred precinct at the time of an Apis funerary procession, with the inner gate visibly closed to ordinary entrants; (3) Theseus and Peirithous making their agreement beside a modest sacred marker near the Athenian sanctuary.
- Orientation strategy: the main panel establishes the physical descent from the Acropolis into lower Athens. Exact locally rendered leaders identify the Acropolis, the sanctuary court, the Serapis image, and the downward route. The relief inset connects Athens to Alexandria and the Nile route to Memphis/Saqqara, with local pins and labels. Captions distinguish Pausanias' claims from reconstruction.
- Approved reference pages: `graphic_book/images/1/1/4.png` and `graphic_book/images/1/1/5.png` for parchment atlas grammar, visual density, painterly sophistication, callouts, and coffee-table-book impact.
- Continuity reference: `graphic_book/images/1/18/3.png` for the current passage-panel and three-inset layout only; it does not supersede the approved anchors.
- Evidence boundary: the Athenian sanctuary's exact plan and location are uncertain, so the scene is explicitly a reconstruction. The Egyptian inset treats Memphis and Saqqara as the linked cult/burial landscape and does not claim that Pausanias supplied modern archaeological precision. The mythic agreement is shown as tradition, not historical reportage.
- Suitability: all figures are fully clothed; the Apis bull is shown ceremonially and without graphic death or embalming detail.
