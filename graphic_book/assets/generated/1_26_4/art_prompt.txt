Pausanias 1.26.4 selected paired statue-portrait design. Two separate near-square upper raster panels, each paired with a lower prose field; all exact text is rendered locally.

Artemis component: polished high-detail painterly historical realism. Close oblique three-quarter view of a modest bronze standing Artemis Leukophryene, fully draped, in a restrained ancient Athenian sanctuary or portico. Aged patina, limestone depth, cool diffuse daylight, verdigris and muted warm bronze. Interpretive lost-statue study; no specific iconographic claim, writing, labels, modern objects, later architecture, nudity or people.

Athena component: polished high-detail painterly historical realism. Low three-quarter view of a fully draped seated Athena attributed by Pausanias to Endoeus, on a simple stone throne in an ancient Athenian sanctuary. Stone carving and faint pigment traces, lavender-grey shade and soft morning light. No writing, labels, later architecture, nudity or living people. The first version showed a Classical Parthenon in the distance, inconsistent with the archaic setting; the accepted edit replaced it with a modest, hazy archaic hillscape. Both versions were reviewed; only the corrected version is used in the page.
