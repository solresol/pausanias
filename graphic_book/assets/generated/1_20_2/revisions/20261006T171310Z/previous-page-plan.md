## Visual design

- **Main visual:** a richly painted oblique interior reconstruction of the nearby temple of Dionysus. The youthful Satyr, fully clothed in a short chiton and offering a drinking-cup, occupies the principal lit niche; Thymilos' separate standing group of fully draped Eros and Dionysus is clearly visible in a second niche. Warm daylight, aged painted plaster, bronze fittings, stone textures, votive vessels, and glimpses of the Athenian slope make the sanctuary feel inhabited and historically grounded. No text is baked into the art.
- **Inset 1:** the immediate end of the workshop stratagem: fully clothed Praxiteles, now reassured, stands beside the two prized works while Phryne deliberately indicates the Eros. The scene reads as a choice, not an emergency or romance.
- **Inset 2:** an oblique exterior approach to a compact Dionysus temple off the Street of the Tripods, with choragic monuments and the Acropolis visible beyond. This provides local orientation without turning the page into a diagram.
- **Orientation strategy:** locally rendered leaders distinguish Praxiteles' youthful Satyr from Thymilos' Eros-and-Dionysus group, identify the drinking-cup, and connect the sanctuary approach to the Street of the Tripods and the Acropolis. A restrained orientation strip places the episode in the Dionysus precinct below the Acropolis.
