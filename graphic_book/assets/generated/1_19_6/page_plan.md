# Page plan — Pausanias 1.19.6

## Passage

- **Passage ID:** 1.19.6
- **Key places:** the Ilisos, Agrai on its far bank, the sanctuary of Artemis Agrotera, the white-marble stadium of Herodes, and Mount Pentelicus.
- **Key events and objects:** Artemis's first hunt after arriving from Delos; the bow held by her cult statue; the crescent-shaped hill descending toward the river in two arms; and the immense quantity of Pentelic marble used for the stadium.

## Visual design

- **Main visual:** a richly painted oblique reconstruction of second-century CE Agrai, looking across the Ilisos toward the white-marble stadium fitted into the crescent hillside. The stadium is the dominant spatial landmark; the modest Artemis Agrotera temple sits nearby, while Athens and the Acropolis establish the crossing point and Mount Pentelicus rises in the distance. No text is baked into the art.
- **Inset 1:** fully clothed Artemis beginning her first hunt after Delos in an Attic river-valley landscape, bow drawn toward a deer moving through brush. The scene emphasizes the bow and hunt without depicting injury.
- **Inset 2:** workers at the Pentelic marble quarries separating and lowering a large white block, with oxen and a timber sledge ready for transport toward Athens. The quarry face and logistics make the stadium's material cost legible.
- **Orientation strategy:** locally rendered leaders identify Athens/Acropolis, the Ilisos crossing, Agrai, Artemis Agrotera's temple, the stadium's two descending arms, and distant Mount Pentelicus. A local orientation strip links Pentelicus to Agrai and the Ilisos.

## Evidence and suitability

- Pausanias supplies the named places, Artemis tradition, bowed statue, white-marble stadium, crescent hill, Herodes, and Pentelic marble claim. Exact building forms, viewpoint, river crossing, quarry operations, transport equipment, vegetation, clothing, and skyline are reconstructed.
- Artemis and all workers are fully clothed. The hunt shows no blood, wound, or struck animal. Generated art contains no exact text; passage text, captions, labels, and leaders are added locally with measured layout.
