1. **Selected: close architectural portrait at upper left, chronological prelude beside it, a subordinate Salamis coast view below that prelude, and two ordered lower prose fields.** The fort scene locates Panactum at the Attic frontier; the smaller shore scene orients Salamis without staging an unrecorded battle. The lower fields carry Cassander's actions and the change of Demetrius. This large image/text silhouette differs from the two preceding pages and appears once in the candidate-plus-five window. The text beside the art is on the right; there is no tall left translation sidebar.
