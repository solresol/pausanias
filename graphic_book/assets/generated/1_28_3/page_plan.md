## Subject, orientation and art direction

One sophisticated painterly raster: a conjectural early Acropolis circuit wall,
not named builders at work and not a measured reconstruction. Low outside
three-quarter view of immense irregular limestone blocks atop natural pale
Acropolis bedrock. Wall recedes along a long rocky ledge, with a restrained
flat-topped citadel profile and broad dry Attic plain glimpsed to one side;
small simple ancient dwellings sit below the rock. No sea against the citadel,
no second Acropolis, fantasy castle, Lion Gate, medieval towers, modern ruins,
scaffolding or precisely reconstructed temple precinct. No scene of construction
or migration, no portrait of Agrolas/Hyperbius, no false proof of Pelasgian
ethnicity. Exact prose retains Cimon, Sicily and Acarnania without invented
travel routes. Caption explicitly marks masonry, setting and appearance
conjectural and builders as literary tradition.

Wide low oblique environmental architecture, wall close at one end but readable
as a long circuit, richly textured irregular stones, joints, lichens, fractured
bedrock, sparse thyme and depth across the plain. Slate/plum-grey cloud,
chalk-grey limestone with restrained rust/umber mineral tones and silvery
late-afternoon light after passing cloud; no golden-ochre wash. Art aspect
2328:1220, no lettering or text holes; exact text entirely outside the raster.
No people or scenery insets are needed. Header names Athens/Acropolis.

Relative to 1.28.2: sculpture/clear cobalt sky becomes environmental masonry/
slate-plum atmosphere, discrete bronze objects become continuous wall and rock,
clear hard sunlight becomes cool filtered light. Camera distance, palette,
lighting and architecture/landscape balance change for the passage. At least
